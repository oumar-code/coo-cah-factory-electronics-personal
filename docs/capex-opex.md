## 7. Payback & Return Analysis

| Metric                          | Value            | Assumptions                                                  |
|---------------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Phase 1 CapEx                   | ₦28.0B           | —                                                            |
| Annual EBITDA (Year 2 steady state) | ₦12.6B        | 25.6% EBITDA margin on ₦49.2B revenue                        |
