    Personal Electronics Factory — CapEx, OpEx & Financial Model¶

    Project Coo-Cah | AI-Powered Manufacturing Ecosystem Factory: Coo-Cah Personal Electronics Factory | Location: Sagamu Industrial Estate, Ogun State | Phase: Phase 1 Document Version: 1.0 | Owner: Finance & Strategy Team

    1. Phase 1 Capital Expenditure Summary¶

    Total Phase 1 CapEx: ₦28.0 Billion (at ₦1,600/USD exchange rate)

    # CapEx Category ₦ Billion % of Total USD Equivalent Notes
    1 Land & Civil Works (18,000 m²) 7.20 25.7% $4.5M Land purchase, site development, drainage, services, building construction
    2 Production Equipment 11.20 40.0% $7.0M 2× SMT lines (DEK/JUKI/Heller/Koh Young), phone assembly, TWS/watch, power bank, RF lab, final QC
    3 Material Handling (AMR + Conveyor + Racking) 2.10 7.5% $1.3M 16× MiR AMRs, conveyor systems, VLMs ×4, pallet racking 200 bays

    5 IT / MES / Automation Systems 1.60 5.7% $1.0M MES deployment, edge nodes, industrial Wi-Fi, panel PCs, CCTV, OPC-UA gateways

    Total 28.00 100% $17.5M
