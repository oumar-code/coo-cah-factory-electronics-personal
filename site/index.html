    Personal Electronics Factory — Executive Summary¶

    Project Coo-Cah | AI-Powered Manufacturing Ecosystem Vertical: Electronics — Consumer Personal Devices | Location: Sagamu Industrial Estate, Ogun State, Nigeria | Phase: Phase 1 (Priority) Document Version: 1.0 | Status: In Development

    1. Factory Overview¶

    The Coo-Cah Personal Electronics Factory is the largest Phase 1 priority facility in the Coo-Cah Electronics vertical, and one of the most strategically important factories in the entire ecosystem. Located on an 18,000 m² site in Sagamu Industrial Estate, Ogun State — adjacent to the Coo-Cah Garage & Power Electronics Factory — this facility manufactures smartphones, feature phones, true wireless earbuds, smartwatches, power banks, and accessories targeted at the African mass market.

    Nigeria alone has over 200 million mobile subscribers, with a device replacement cycle averaging 18–24 months. The West African region represents one of the fastest-growing consumer electronics markets globally, driven by a young population, increasing mobile money adoption, and a shift from feature phones to smartphones. Coo-Cah's strategy: manufacture quality devices locally, eliminate import markups, provide local warranty support, and deliver products at price points that African consumers can afford.

    The factory is designed as a fully MES-integrated, AI-monitored smart facility from day one. Two full SMT production lines, phone and TWS/watch assembly lines, RF test chambers for NCC type approval compliance, and a complete digital twin infrastructure position this facility for progressive automation through to lights-out operation on the SMT lines by Phase 3.

    2.2 Product Descriptions¶

    CCE-FP-3G / CCE-FP-4G (Feature Phones): Dual-SIM feature phones targeting the largest segment of the Nigerian market — first-time phone owners, rural consumers, and secondary devices. 2.8" QVGA screen, 2,000mAh removable battery, torch, FM radio, and long standby time. The 4G variant adds VoLTE for voice continuity on LTE networks. Designed for durability in hot, humid conditions, with rubber-sealed ports. Sold at highly competitive price points to contest the market dominated by Chinese OEM imports.

    CCE-SP-LITE (Budget Smartphone): 4G LTE smartphone targeting the under-₦90,000 price tier. 6.1" IPS LCD display, MediaTek Helio G85 SoC, 3GB RAM + 32GB storage (expandable), 13MP main camera, 4,000mAh battery with 10W charging, USB-C. Runs Android 14 (Go Edition) with Coo-Cah UI layer. Passes NCC Type Approval and IEC 62368-1.

    CCE-SP-MID (Mid Smartphone): 4G/5G smartphone targeting the ₦120,000–₦200,000 segment. 6.5" AMOLED display, MediaTek Dimensity 6020 or Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 2, 6GB/128GB, 50MP triple camera, 4,500mAh + 33W fast charge, USB-C. Designed to compete directly with imported Tecno, Infinix, and Xiaomi mid-range devices on both performance and local brand trust.

    CCE-TWS-01 / CCE-TWS-PRO (True Wireless Earbuds): Stereo Bluetooth 5.3 earbuds in a charging case. CCE-TWS-01: 6h playback + 24h case, IPX4, touch controls. CCE-TWS-PRO adds active noise cancellation, 8h + 32h, IPX5, and a premium semi-in-ear fit. Both pass NCC Type Approval for Bluetooth devices.

    CCE-SW-LITE / CCE-SW-PRO (Smartwatches): The Lite model is a fitness band with a 1.4" TFT colour display, 7-day battery, heart rate, SpO₂, step counter. The Pro model is a full smartwatch with a 1.7" AMOLED, GPS, 5 ATM water resistance, 5-day battery, and NFC for mobile payments. Both connect via Bluetooth 5.0 to the Coo-Cah Smart App (iOS + Android).

    CCE-PB Series (Power Banks): 5,000mAh, 10,000mAh, and 20,000mAh models with USB-A + USB-C output. The 20K model includes 18W PD fast charging. High-cycle LiFePO₄ cells selected for the 10K and 20K models for safety in Nigerian climate conditions. All models pass IEC 62133 safety and NCC certification.
